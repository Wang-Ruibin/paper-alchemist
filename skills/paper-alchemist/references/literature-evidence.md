# Literature discovery and citation evidence

[简体中文](zh-CN/literature-evidence.md)

## Start with the supplied corpus

Read the user's reference list, notes and accessible papers first. Separate
papers used for writing-style guidance from sources authorized to support the
current argument. Distillation does not automatically add a citation to the
research context. Searching for literature does not authorize changing an
existing style profile; use the incremental workflow for a requested update.

For a literature request, establish the question and important boundaries, then
reuse supplied sources and search only the gaps. Prefer primary publications,
official repositories and publisher records. Use host search tools when
available; local scripts do not query bibliographic services. Send only query
terms needed for discovery rather than private drafts or result tables.

## Keep discovery distinct from verification

For each relevant source, distinguish these observations:

1. Found: a search result or bibliographic entry exists.
2. Metadata checked: title, authors, year, identifier and version agree with an
   authoritative record. Keep preprint and published versions distinct.
3. Content inspected: the relevant passage, table or equation was actually read.
4. Claim assessed: that inspected content supports, contradicts or is
   insufficient for a specific proposed claim at its stated scope.

A DOI resolving or a familiar title is not content verification. An inaccessible
paper stays uninspected; use an accessible abstract only for what it actually
says and record that reading scope. Do not fill a missing author, date, DOI,
result or quotation from model memory. When a relevant correction or retraction
notice is found, preserve it alongside the original source and reassess the
affected claim. Do not claim that every source was screened for notices unless
that check was performed.

## Build a useful evidence matrix

Use a compact matrix with citation key, verified identity/version, study or
problem setting, method, finding, relevant limitation, reading scope and source
locator. Add the proposed manuscript claim and its support assessment. Preserve
exact units, populations, objective directions and experimental conditions.
Use short evidence quotations or source-bound notes, not copied paper prose.

Check whether a source supports the whole attached clause. A paper describing
a method may not document the user's implementation settings; separate those
claims. Conflicting findings belong in the synthesis. A reported association
does not justify causation, a best observed value does not establish an optimal
certificate, and a narrow comparison does not establish universal superiority.
Explain what evidence would change the interpretation instead of strengthening
the desired conclusion without support.

For citation checking of an existing draft, enumerate the cited clauses and
their keys, verify the identity and inspect the supporting locations, then
report unsupported, misattributed, ambiguous or uninspected items with concrete
locations. Proposed new references are suggestions until verified and added to
the user's allowed bibliography; do not silently insert them.

## Match the search to the requested review

A focused narrative review can use an explicit, bounded search record: queries,
databases or tools, date, scope and important coverage gaps. Novelty statements
must reflect that search scope; an empty result does not prove that no work
exists. Structure synthesis around mechanisms, assumptions or evidence rather
than one paragraph per paper.

Only call a review systematic when the requested work includes a defined
protocol and actual execution. Record inclusion/exclusion criteria, search
sources and dates, deduplication rules, study-level decisions with reasons,
unavailable full texts and counts at each step. Distinguish duplicate records
from excluded studies and keep screened, excluded and included totals
reconcilable. Use actual independent screening only when authorized and
available; sequential readings by one Agent are not two blinded screeners.
Do not invent PRISMA compliance or counts from an informal search. If the scope
or coverage cannot be completed, deliver a bounded synthesis and identify the
remaining work.

## Hand evidence to writing

Add only verified, authorized source claims and citation keys to the study
context. Keep unresolved sources in the research notes, outside manuscript
prose. When a persistent audit is requested, record inspected source text,
locators and claim spans using [research-workflow.md](research-workflow.md).
The local checker detects stale files and broken bindings; the Agent still
judges whether the cited evidence entails the sentence.
